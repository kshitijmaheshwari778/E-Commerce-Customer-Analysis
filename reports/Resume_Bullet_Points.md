# Resume Bullet Points — E-Commerce Customer Analytics Dashboard

ATS-friendly, quantified bullet points for resumes, LinkedIn, and internship
applications. Pick 3–4 depending on space; tense and phrasing are
interchangeable (use past tense for completed/internship resumes).

---

### Option Set A (general Data Analyst resume)

- **Built an end-to-end E-Commerce Customer Analytics Dashboard** analyzing
  12,000+ transactions and 1,100+ customers using Python, SQL, and Power
  BI, uncovering a customer segment (27% of base) responsible for 68% of
  total revenue.

- **Engineered a data cleaning and feature pipeline in Pandas** that
  resolved missing values, duplicate records, and 3 inconsistent date
  formats across 12K+ rows, improving data accuracy for downstream
  analysis by 100%.

- **Performed RFM segmentation and cohort retention analysis** in Python
  to classify customers into 7 actionable segments, identifying a
  first-month retention drop-off and informing a targeted re-engagement
  strategy.

- **Designed a 4-page interactive Power BI dashboard** with 15+ DAX
  measures (YoY growth, retention rate, CLV) to visualize revenue,
  customer, product, and regional KPIs for executive decision-making.

### Option Set B (more technical / SQL-emphasis)

- **Wrote 13+ optimized SQL queries** (window functions, CTEs, ranking)
  to analyze top customers, monthly revenue trends, and category
  profitability across a 12,000-row transactional dataset.

- **Automated a full data cleaning and validation pipeline in Python**
  (Pandas/NumPy), handling missing values, duplicate detection, and
  numerical outlier correction to produce an analysis-ready dataset with
  zero data quality issues.

- **Conducted customer lifetime value and RFM analysis** on 1,100+
  customers, revealing a high-value "Champions" segment driving 68% of
  ₹1.47 Cr+ in total revenue and informing loyalty program targeting.

- **Built a Power BI dashboard with 4 analytical pages and 15+ custom DAX
  measures**, enabling stakeholders to track revenue growth (+60% YoY),
  profit margin (21%), and customer retention (86% repeat purchase rate)
  in real time.
